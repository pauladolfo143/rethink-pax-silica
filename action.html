OPTIONAL / FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Q01

Why should students care?

Because semiconductor technology affects everyday life, economies, and strategic interests.

Q02

Does questioning mean opposing?

No. Questioning means examining benefits, risks, transparency, and independence before deciding.

Q03

What should we ask?

Who benefits? Who carries the risks? What commitments are made? How transparent is the agreement?

Q04

How can students help?

Stay informed, discuss responsibly, share reliable information, and encourage critical thinking.
